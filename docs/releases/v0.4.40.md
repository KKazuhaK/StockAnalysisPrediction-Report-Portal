# v0.4.40 — announcements stop hiding themselves

## Upgrade notes

From v0.4.39 — pull and restart. No schema changes and nothing to configure; three new strings per
language, one new site setting that defaults to the new behaviour.

Rolling back to v0.4.39 is clean: the fold switch is a stored setting the older build simply ignores,
returning to the always-folded band it shipped with.

## Changed

**Announcements no longer fold themselves away.** v0.4.39 drew the site-wide band as a single
collapsed line — the leading announcement's title, a `+N` count, expand on click — on the argument
that a band present on *every* page is chrome and has to cost what chrome costs: about 36px against
about 150px for three stacked cards.

Seen in use, that argument does not survive. Folding hides, behind a click, something an operator
decided was worth interrupting everybody with, and a band that conceals its own contents is one
readers stop trusting. The saving was real and bought the wrong thing.

Both bands — the home-page cards and the site-wide strip — now show every announcement addressed to
you, in full.

**Folding becomes a switch instead of a rule.** 管理 → 站点 → 公告 has a site-wide "fold the extra
announcements" toggle, **off by default**. Turn it on and each band shows the first announcement with
the rest behind a "N more" link, which is roughly what v0.4.39 did — the difference is that it is now
your choice. How many announcements a portal runs at once is something only its operator knows, so
this is a setting rather than a decision made for you.

**The site-wide band is tinted by the most severe announcement in it**, not by whichever one is
first. A stack whose only incident notice sits third no longer reads as a calm blue bar.

## Fixed

**The site-wide band drew its first announcement twice when expanded.** The collapsed line stayed
visible after expanding *and* the list beneath it repeated every announcement, including the one
already shown — so the band looked broken exactly when somebody had opened it to read something.
While folded it now shows the lead and nothing else.

**Both switches on the announcements page were unlabelled.** There are two of them now — the per-row
enable and the site-wide fold — and neither carried an accessible name, so a screen reader announced
two anonymous switches. Both are named.

## Verification

Web typecheck, 498 tests across 73 files, and the SPA build. Go build, vet, gofmt and the full Go
suite.

New coverage: that both bands show everything until the fold switch is turned on; that a folded strip
never draws its lead twice once expanded; and that the fold switch survives an unrelated settings
save — it rides the site-settings endpoint, whose per-field merge is the only reason it did not need
an endpoint of its own, so that property is pinned rather than assumed.
