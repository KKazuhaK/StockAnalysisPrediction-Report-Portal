# v0.4.40 — an announcement that follows the reader

## Upgrade notes

From v0.4.39 — pull and restart. No schema changes: the column this release turns on was already
declared and every existing announcement already carries its default. Nothing to configure; four new
strings per language.

Rolling back to v0.4.39 is clean. An announcement you set to show on every page reverts to showing
on the home page only, because that is what the older build draws; the stored value is untouched, so
rolling forward again restores it.

## Added

**An announcement can now show on every page, not only the home page.** The editor has a "where it
shows" choice: *Home only* — the original behaviour, a full card on the home page — or *Every page*,
which puts the announcement in front of a reader wherever they are.

The two look different on purpose. The home-page card is the roomy one, on a page somebody visits
deliberately. A site-wide announcement follows the reader everywhere, so it is chrome and costs what
chrome costs: **one tinted line under the header** — level icon, the leading announcement's title, a
`+N` count when there are more — that expands in place when clicked. Three stacked cards would take
about 150px off every screen in the portal against about 36px for the strip, and a band that
expensive is one people learn to scroll past, which forfeits the whole point of having it.

The list marks a site-wide announcement with a tag, so which ones follow readers around is visible
without opening each one.

Neither surface appears in the admin console or on the mobile chat page. Both are deliberately
stripped layouts — chat hides the header to give the thread and composer the viewport, and the
console is full-bleed with a rail measured off the header height — and a band on either adds a
scrollbar with nothing to scroll.

## Notes

**"Everyone" includes external accounts, and that is deliberate.** The announcements that most need
to reach everybody — a maintenance window, a data source that is down — affect an external tenant
exactly as much as an internal colleague, and the tenant who was not told is the one who files a
support ticket. When the audience picker arrives in the next release, "internal only" will be
expressed by addressing the group internal accounts belong to, not by a separate setting.

The reader side of per-group targeting is built and tested but has no editing UI yet; every
announcement is addressed to everyone until the next release. See
`docs/adr/0025-site-announcements.md`.

## Fixed

**The pre-upgrade "don't show again" could have been thrown away.** Splitting the reader into three
surfaces — the site-wide strip, the home-page card, the popup — meant each saw only the
announcements it draws, while the one-time migration of the old dismissal ran from whichever of them
rendered first. The old key holds a bare hash with no id beside it and is consumed when read, so
matching it against a partial list, or against nothing at all before the first fetch landed, would
have discarded it unmatched and re-interrupted every reader who had already dismissed the
announcement. The migration now runs once, over the whole feed, and refuses to run at all until
there is something to match against. Caught before release; v0.4.39 is unaffected.

**The zh-TW locale keeps up with removals.** Carried over from v0.4.39's generator change and
exercised again here.

## Verification

Web typecheck, 485 tests across 73 files, and the SPA build. Go build, vet, gofmt and the full Go
suite — no Go changed in this release, which is what running them proves. New coverage: that each
announcement is drawn once by the surface that owns its scope rather than twice on the home page,
that the site-wide strip collapses to one line and expands in place, that a dismissible strip
closes, and that the legacy-dismissal migration refuses an empty feed and drops a key that matches
nothing rather than retrying forever.
