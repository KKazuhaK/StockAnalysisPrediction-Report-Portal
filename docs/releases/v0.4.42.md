# v0.4.42 — an announcement that reaches nobody says so

## Upgrade notes

From v0.4.41 — pull and restart. No schema changes and nothing to configure; eleven new strings per
language, one removed.

**One API contract changes.** `DELETE /api/admin/groups/{id}` now answers **409** when deleting the
group would leave an announcement addressed to nobody, until the caller says what should happen:
`?orphans=disable` switches those announcements off, `?orphans=keep` leaves them. Deletes that
orphan nothing are unaffected. The admin console asks and passes the answer, so nothing changes for
somebody clicking through the UI; a script that deletes groups will need the parameter, and that is
the point — the silent path is the one being closed.

Rolling back to v0.4.41 is clean.

## Changed

**The 50-announcement limit is gone.** Refusing a save at the moment somebody most needs to
broadcast does not stop readers from ignoring an overcrowded band — it just stops the broadcast. The
console warns instead: past five announcements **live at once**, a yellow bar suggests merging some,
giving the older ones an end time, or switching a few off. It counts what readers actually face, not
the draft pile, so twenty drafts and two live announcements say nothing. Readers were already
protected by the band folding its overflow behind a "N more" counter.

## Added

**An announcement that reaches nobody is flagged as such.** A targeted announcement cannot be
*created* with an empty recipient list — the form and the server both refuse — but deleting the
group it was addressed to produces the same state afterwards. Until now that announcement kept
reporting itself as live: enabled, inside its window, green tag, reaching no one. It now shows a red
**"reaches nobody"** status with a tooltip saying what to do about it (pick recipients again, switch
to Everyone, or turn it off). This catches the state however it arose, including account deletion or
a change made straight through the API.

**Deleting a group tells you what it will do to the announcements first.** If any announcement is
addressed to the group you are deleting, a dialog lists them before anything happens and separates
the two cases that matter: the ones that **will reach nobody** (this group was their only recipient)
from the ones that **keep working** (they have others). You then choose whether the orphaned ones
should be switched off — the recommended answer, so that nothing keeps pretending to be published —
or kept as they are, where the announcement list flags them.

Neither answer is applied for you. Choosing on your behalf would be choosing whether a published
announcement quietly stops or quietly lies.

## Notes

Deleting an account does not block the same way. A group is the targeting unit and deleting one is
rare and high-impact; deleting an account is routine, already carries a long list of consequences,
and has a bulk path. The "reaches nobody" flag covers it.

## Verification

Web typecheck, 496 tests across 73 files, and the SPA build. Go build, vet, gofmt and the full Go
suite. New coverage: that the count is genuinely uncapped (sixty announcements save and all sixty
reach a reader); that the crowding warning counts live announcements and ignores drafts; that a
zero-recipient announcement reads as unreachable rather than live, and a merely switched-off one
does not; that deleting a group refuses without a decision and does not half-apply, that `disable`
switches the orphan off while `keep` leaves it exactly as it was, and that deleting one of several
recipients needs no decision at all; and that the preflight marks orphans apart from announcements
that keep working.
